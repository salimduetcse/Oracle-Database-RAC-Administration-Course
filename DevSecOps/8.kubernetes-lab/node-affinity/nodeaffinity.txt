nginx-affinity.yaml

apiVersion: apps/v1
kind: Deployment
metadata:
  name: nginx-with-affinity
spec:
  replicas: 1
  selector:
    matchLabels:
      app: nginx-affinity
  template:
    metadata:
      labels:
        app: nginx-affinity
    spec:
      affinity:
        nodeAffinity:
          requiredDuringSchedulingIgnoredDuringExecution:
            nodeSelectorTerms:
            - matchExpressions:
              - key: node-type
                operator: In
                values:
                - worker
      containers:
      - name: nginx
        image: nginx:latest
        ports:
        - containerPort: 80

Use preferred instead of required (soft rule)
If you want to prefer a node but allow fallback to others, use:



preferredDuringSchedulingIgnoredDuringExecution:
  - weight: 1
    preference:
      matchExpressions:
      - key: node-type
        operator: In
        values:
        - worker
